turn your framework 12 into a creaky wooden door, basically [this](https://github.com/samhenrigold/LidAngleSensor) (that was also the inspiration too) but ported to a fw12.

turns out framework 12s have a surprisingly accurate lid angle sensor. while normally used for detecting tablet mode, you can also use it for other fun stuff.
